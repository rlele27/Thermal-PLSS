REHEATER SAMPLE COUNT
29,760 entries are intentional, not an array expansion error. Each of the
960 intervals stores 30 internal one-second control samples plus one endpoint.
The reheater is sized at these instantaneous states; plots use 960 interval
averages. This sample collection and sizing logic are unchanged from before
the restructure. No reduction/downsampling was applied.
